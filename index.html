A repeatable study session

25 minutes. One concept. One proof.

Use this small routine instead of passive reading. It works for GL, Payables, Procurement, Security, Integrations, and more.

  1. 5 minExplain the business problem in plain English.
  2. 10 minTrace the process and identify the Oracle objects involved.
  3. 5 minWrite one test scenario with expected results.
  4. 5 minRecall the idea without looking, then note what remains unclear.
